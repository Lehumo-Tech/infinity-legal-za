📖 How to Use

1. Access the Application

Open the application in your browser using the Preview Panel on the right, or click "Open in New Tab" for a full-screen experience.

2. Login

Use any of the credentials above. For a full management experience, start with md@infinitylegal.co.za. For a paralegal view, use paralegal@infinitylegal.co.za. For HR, use hr@infinitylegal.co.za.

3. Navigate the Intranet

  • Workbench — Your personalized dashboard with KPIs, quick actions, and recent activity
  • Cases — View and manage all legal cases
  • Leads — Track potential clients from inquiry to conversion
  • Documents — Upload, review, and manage legal documents
  • Consultations — Schedule and log client consultations
  • Tasks — Manage your assigned tasks and deadlines
  • Staff Portal — Access HR, Sales, Paralegal, and Management sub-portals
  • Org Structure — View the firm's hierarchical chart
  • Analytics — View firm-wide performance metrics (management only)

4. Test Different Roles

Log out and log in with different credentials to see how the interface adapts:

  • Managing Director — Full access to all features
  • HR Manager — Staff portal + HR sub-portal
  • Receptionist — Sales portal + lead management
  • Paralegal — Paralegal portal + document upload
  • Client — Limited view with pricing info
